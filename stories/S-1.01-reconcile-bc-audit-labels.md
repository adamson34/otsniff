- **So that** spec-to-source traceability isn't silently broken

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001 (traces to ASR-001 finding)
- BC-INDEX BC-AUDIT-005 reads "DHCP option walk is bounded and length-checked"
  matching `otsniff-coverage-audit.md` row 5.
- BC-INDEX BC-AUDIT-006 reads "DHCP 3-tier IP resolution (yiaddr / ciaddr / option 50)"
  matching `otsniff-coverage-audit.md` row 6.

  not scoped to dns_resolver.

  matching the audit source.
- BC-INDEX BC-AUDIT-011 reads "stale_tls is_stale range is 0x0300..=0x0302"
  matching the audit source.
- BC-INDEX BC-AUDIT-012 reads "engineering_commands rolls up by (src, dst) pair"
  matching the audit source.

### AC-002 (traces to ASR-005 — evidence cap is wrong for unexpected_protocols)
- BC-AUDIT-008 is rewritten to read: "Evidence cap of 15 rows per
  finding (general invariant). Exception: `unexpected_protocols` caps
  at **5 per label** (see `src/findings/unexpected_protocols.rs:70`
  `bucket.len() < 5`), so total evidence rows for that finding can be
  up to `5 × labels_observed`."

  separate BC-AUDIT-016, to avoid renumbering downstream references.
