# S-5.01: Periodic parse-loop progress in `-v` mode

## Narrative

- **As a** user running otsniff on a multi-GB capture
- **I want** periodic progress output when `-v` is set
- **So that** I don't wonder whether the process hung

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001 (BC-9.04.001 — progress emission cadence)
- When `-v` is set, every N=100,000 packets (configurable via const)
  OR every 10 MB read, otsniff emits a stderr line of the form:
  `[parse] processed 2,500,000 packets / 1.2 GB ...`.
- The final summary line is unchanged.
- **Test:** A unit test feeds a synthetic 500,000-packet iterator and
  asserts ≥ 4 progress emissions plus 1 final line (via capturing
  stderr or injecting a writer).

### AC-002 (no output in non-verbose mode)
- Without `-v`, no progress lines are emitted.
- **Test:** Smoke test in `tests/cli_smoke.rs` confirms.

### AC-003 (progress is rate-limited)
- Progress emissions are at most once per 2 seconds even on very fast
  captures, to avoid swamping stderr on small fast files.

## Architecture Mapping

| Component | Module | Pure/Effectful |
|-----------|--------|---------------|
| Parse loop | `src/pcap.rs::iter_packets` (or wrapper) | effectful-shell |
| Progress emitter | new helper | effectful-shell |

## Edge Cases

| ID | Scenario | Expected Behavior |
|----|----------|-------------------|
| EC-001 | Stderr redirected to /dev/null | Progress emissions silently dropped |
| EC-002 | Single-packet PCAP | No progress emissions; just final summary |
| EC-003 | `-v` but no TTY | Output still goes to stderr |
